The past weekend, we went for a swim in the swimming pool. The day before (Friday),
would have been when Vista Harmoni Residence's management cleaned up the pool (they
have a notice board that says they close the pool for maintenance and cleaning every
Monday, Wednesday and Friday from 12:00pm - 3:00pm).

Me and my wife and kids went for a swim, and to our surprise, the management left
some treasure for us to find! How fun! I imagine they must have left them there on purpose,
seeing as how we went on the Saturday just one day after maintenance. Wow! It's quite
amazing that they would give us this extra activity and making swimming in the pool
that much more fun!

Well, every great adventure starts somewhere, and ours started right here. I didn't wear
my goggles that day (mainly because I didn't wanna put on contact lenses), but my kids did!
And boy did they have fun treasure hunting, diving into the Vista Harmoni Residences swimming
pool with sharp eyes and empty pockets, and came back with a whole haul of finds to sort through.
